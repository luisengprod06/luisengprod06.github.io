Projects

Coursework from the MSc in Statistics at UFPE, done in groups and implemented in R. Hover over or tap the charts to see the values.

Spam detection in e-mails

Multivariate Analysis · supervised classification

Comparison of KNN, logistic regression, LDA and QDA on the Spambase data, with 57 variables. Models were fitted on a training set and evaluated on an independent test set. Logistic regression had the best overall performance, with an AUC of 0.972.

Regularization in polynomial regression

Data Science · simulation

A simulation study comparing Ridge, Lasso and Elastic Net on high-degree polynomials, with a sensitivity analysis to noise. With highly collinear predictors, Lasso and Elastic Net outperformed Ridge, but this advantage nearly vanishes when the noise is high.

Statistics for circular data

Data Science · von Mises and bootstrap

Movement directions of 76 turtles and 100 ants, analyzed with uniformity tests, von Mises fitting, bootstrap and Monte Carlo simulation. For the turtles, a mixture of two von Mises distributions fit clearly better than the other models.

Financial well-being index

Multivariate Analysis · principal components

Principal component analysis of a financial well-being scale (National Financial Well-Being Survey). The first component captured 56.1% of the variance and was interpreted as a financial stability index.

Classification methods: a comparative study

Data Science · machine learning

LDA, QDA, logistic regression, KNN and RDA compared on the Iris data and in simulations with different coefficients. On Iris, LDA, logistic regression and RDA tied for the highest accuracy. In the simulations, KNN had the lowest mean accuracies.

Power of Hotelling's T² test

Multivariate Analysis · simulation

A simulation with 1,000 replicates to measure the test's power for samples of 30 to 1,000 observations. With no shift, the rejection rate stays close to the expected 5%. With a shift, power grows with sample size, and faster for larger shifts.

Other work: a simulation check that, under multivariate normality, the squared Mahalanobis distance follows a chi-square distribution, with closer agreement as the sample grows.
