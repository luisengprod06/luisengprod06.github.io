Uma aproximação escalada para DCT de comprimento 16 [A scaled approximation for the length-16 DCT]

L. M. F. de Oliveira and R. J. Cintra. Proceeding Series of the Brazilian Society of Computational and Applied Mathematics, v. 11, n. 1, 2025. Presented at the XLIII CNMAC, Porto de Galinhas (PE), Brazil, 2024. In Portuguese.

Low-complexity approximations of the discrete cosine transform, with entries in {0, ±1, ±2}, for Internet of Things devices. An angular-similarity search over matrix rows selected 8,072,064 candidate matrices. The optimal matrix for N = 8 had the lowest mean squared error among the compared approximations and was scaled up to N = 16.
